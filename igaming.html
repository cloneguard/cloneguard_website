FAQ

iGaming brand protection, answered plainly.

How do I know if someone is cloning my casino site?

Common signs are new domains reproducing your design, ads running under your brand name that you didn't buy, or app store listings you don't recognize. Continuous monitoring catches these before players report them to you.

Can a cloned casino site actually be taken down?

Yes. Hosting providers, registrars, and ad networks will act on a properly evidenced takedown request, and a registered trademark makes that request considerably stronger.

What if the clone is hosted outside my licensing jurisdiction?

Cross-border takedowns are slower but not impossible. We pursue the registrar, host, and any ad network involved regardless of where the clone is hosted.

Does trademark registration actually stop clone sites?

Not on its own, but it gives every takedown and enforcement action real legal standing, which speeds up how fast hosts and platforms act.

How fast can a fake casino app be removed from an app store?

Verified cases are typically actioned within 24 hours of confirmation, though app store review times vary by platform.
