About

Built for the industries other vendors overlook.

iGaming and Crypto brands get impersonated constantly, and most brand protection vendors treat them as too complicated, too regulated, or too risky to bother with. We built CloneGuard specifically for that gap.

Why we exist

Mainstream vendors are slow to serve these industries. We aren't.

Generalist brand protection vendors are built for retail, media, and consumer tech. When a casino or a crypto exchange comes to them, the compliance questions alone can take weeks. We work exclusively with iGaming and Crypto brands, so there's no ramp-up explaining what a KYC flow is or why a licensing regulator matters.

How we work

Protection before the problem. Enforcement after it.

Every engagement covers two directions: protection that stops problems before they cost you a customer (trademark registration, continuous monitoring, domain and IP strategy), and enforcement that acts the moment a threat is confirmed (takedowns, cease and desist letters, negotiations, domain disputes). Most vendors do one or the other. We run both from a single team.

How filing and enforcement get done

One point of contact. Specialist execution behind it.

Trademark filings and enforcement actions are carried out with a vetted network of specialist legal and enforcement partners. You deal with one team throughout. The specialist work happens behind that relationship, not scattered across law firms and vendors you have to manage yourself.

Focus

iGaming and Crypto, specifically.

No generalist checklist, no "we cover every industry" claim. Every case we handle sits inside one of two verticals, which is what lets us move fast on the threats that are actually common there.
