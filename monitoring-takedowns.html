FAQ

Monitoring & Takedowns, answered plainly.

What's the difference between monitoring and takedowns?

Monitoring is continuous detection across web, apps, and social. Takedowns are the enforcement action once a case is verified - getting the confirmed clone or fake account removed. You get both as part of the same service; monitoring runs continuously and takedowns are metered into your plan.

Can I buy takedowns without a monitoring subscription?

Yes. Single takedowns and takedown packs are available as one-off purchases, quoted directly without a Protection Plan subscription.

Can I get monitoring without takedown execution, or vice versa?

Yes, on request. If you already run detection or enforcement in-house, we can scope and quote just the half you're missing.

How fast is a confirmed case actioned?

Typically within 24 hours of confirmation, though response time varies by host, registrar, or platform.

What happens if a host or platform doesn't respond?

We escalate. Persistent cases move to Brand Enforcement - formal legal notices and dispute proceedings - to force resolution.

Do you monitor app stores and messaging apps, or just websites?

Both. Coverage spans casino and exchange sites, app stores, social platforms, and messaging apps like Telegram and Discord.

How do you avoid false positives?

Every flagged case is reviewed by a human analyst before any action is taken - nothing is actioned automatically.

What do I get in reporting?

A clear monthly report covering every case detected and actioned - no dashboard login required.
