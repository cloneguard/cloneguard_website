FAQ

Specialist services, answered plainly.

How is Specialist Services different from a Protection Plan?

Protection Plans cover monitoring, takedowns, and trademark filing. Specialist Services are introduced separately, on request, when your business needs executive protection, insurance placement, or deeper IP strategy.

Do you provide cyber insurance directly?

No. We introduce you to specialist brokers who underwrite for iGaming and Crypto risk specifically, and stay involved as your point of contact.

Can I get Specialist Services without a Protection Plan?

Yes. These are available on their own, quoted individually based on what you need.

How fast can executive protection be put in place?

An initial exposure review typically starts within days of your request. Ongoing monitoring and takedown coverage for an executive's identity is scoped and confirmed from there.

Who are the specialist partners you work with?

We work with vetted brokers and specialists selected for iGaming and Crypto risk specifically. We stay your single point of contact throughout, so you're never managing multiple vendor relationships directly.

Does Domain & IP Portfolio Strategy include filing new trademarks?

It can. Strategy work identifies the gaps, and any resulting trademark filings are handled through our Trademark Registration service under the same point of contact.
