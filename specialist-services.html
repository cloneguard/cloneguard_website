How is Specialist Services different from a Protection Plan?

Protection Plans cover monitoring, takedowns, and trademark filing. Specialist Services are introduced separately, on request, when your business needs executive protection, insurance placement, or deeper IP strategy.

Do you provide cyber insurance directly?

No. We introduce you to specialist brokers who underwrite for iGaming and Crypto risk specifically, and stay involved as your point of contact.

Can I get Specialist Services without a Protection Plan?

Yes. These are available on their own, quoted individually based on what you need.
