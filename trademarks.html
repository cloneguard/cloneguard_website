Why speed matters

Filing early doesn't just protect the name. It's what makes every takedown after that enforceable.

Most trademark registers reward whoever files first, not whoever used the name first. In iGaming and Crypto, where a live brand is worth copying fast, that gap gets exploited.

First-to-file, not first-to-use

In the UK, EU, and most jurisdictions, someone else can file for your name before you and legally hold the right to it, even if you built the brand.

The filing date is what's protected

Full registration takes 4 to 18 months, but the date you filed is what counts, protected from day one, while examination is still running.

It's what makes takedowns enforceable

A takedown backed by a registered mark is a legal right a platform has to act on. Without one, it's a request they can ignore.

Filing it right

A template form doesn't know what class your business actually operates in.

Generic filing services are built for any business. iGaming and Crypto brands run into specification, class, and registrability issues those services aren't built to catch.

What to expect

The clock starts on the day you file, not the day you're registered.

Timelines vary by register and whether anyone objects, but the shape is broadly the same everywhere.

Day 0

Filed

Your filing date is what's protected from this point, even while examination is still ongoing.

Month 2 to 3

Published

The application is published on the register, opening a window for third parties to raise an objection.

Month 3 to 4

Opposition window

If no one objects in this window, the application moves forward to registration.

Month 4 to 18

Registered

Certificate issued. Valid for 10 years, and renewable indefinitely after that.

Where to file

Register where you're actually licensed and operating, not just where it's simplest to file.

The right jurisdiction depends on where your licence sits and where your players are, not a default. These are the markets we see iGaming and Crypto brands need most often.

United Kingdom - UKIPO

United Kingdom

One of the largest regulated online gambling markets in the world, with its own register independent of the EU since Brexit. The default first filing for any brand touching UK-facing players.

European Union - EUIPO

European Union

A single filing covers all 27 member states in one registration, including Malta, home to the MGA and the jurisdiction most iGaming operators are actually licensed in. One of the most efficient filings available for this sector.

100+ jurisdictions - Madrid Protocol

Rest of world

Direct filing in over 100 jurisdictions, including the UAE, anchored by Dubai's VARA framework for Web3 and Crypto brands. A single base filing can extend to 130+ countries through the Madrid Protocol. On the US specifically: real-money gambling faces registrability restrictions at the USPTO that don't apply to most Crypto and fintech brands, so we check that with you first.
