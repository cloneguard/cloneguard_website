FAQ

Plan questions, answered plainly.

What happens if I go over my included takedowns?

Extra takedowns are billed at the discounted top-up rate for your tier, or you can request a one-off pack. There is no automatic overage charge.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. You can move up or down between Foundation, Growth, and Enterprise at your next billing cycle.

Is trademark filing really included?

One trademark filing is included with any annual subscription. Monthly billing does not include a filing.

What currencies can I pay in?

USD, GBP, and EUR. Prices are converted automatically at checkout.

What if I need something that isn't covered by my plan?

Send us details of what you need. We quote it separately and apply your subscriber discount where it applies.
