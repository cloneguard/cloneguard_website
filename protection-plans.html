FAQ

Plan questions, answered plainly.

What's the difference between Monitor and the bundled plans?

Monitor is pay-as-you-go: full monitoring coverage with no monthly takedown commitment, billed per takedown as they happen. Growth and Scale bundle a monthly takedown allowance at a lower per-unit rate, which works out cheaper once you're actioning more than the occasional case.

Are included takedowns shared across all my brands, or per brand?

Shared account-wide. Adding a brand adds monitoring coverage for that brand, it does not add to your included takedown allowance.

What happens if I go over my included takedowns?

Billed at your tier's overage rate ($79 on Growth, $59 on Scale), or you can request a one-off pack. There is no automatic overage charge.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. You can move between Monitor, Growth, Scale, and Enterprise at your next billing cycle.

Is trademark filing included in my plan?

Not bundled in, deliberately, so you're never paying for a filing you don't need. Annual subscribers get a discount instead: 10% off on Monitor, 20% off on Growth, 30% off on Scale. Enterprise has filing scoped into the contract directly.

How is pricing worked out for additional brands?

Each brand needs its own monitoring coverage, so additional brands are priced per brand. On Monitor every brand is priced the same; on Growth and Scale, the per-brand rate gets cheaper as you move up a tier.

What currencies can I pay in?

USD, GBP, and EUR. Prices are converted automatically at checkout.

What if I need something that isn't covered by my plan?

Send us details of what you need. We quote it separately and apply your subscriber discount where it applies.
