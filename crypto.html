What's targeting your brand

Four ways exchanges and platforms get impersonated.

Lookalike exchange domains

Near-identical domains served through paid search ads or single-character URL typos, harvesting real login credentials and draining wallets directly - the highest-volume threat by funds lost.

Fake wallet browser extensions

Extensions disguised as legitimate tools that inject malicious code and alter transactions once connected to a wallet.

Airdrop & project impersonation phishing

Fake airdrop campaigns and impersonated project accounts luring a wallet connect or seed phrase.

Fake support & deepfake impersonation

Fake support accounts and AI-swapped face and voice on Telegram or Zoom calls - fewer users hit, but the reputational damage travels fast once one goes public.

FAQ

Crypto brand protection, answered plainly.

How do I know if someone is impersonating my exchange?

Lookalike domains showing up in search ads, fake support accounts on Telegram or Discord, and unfamiliar browser extensions using your name are the most common signs. Continuous monitoring catches these before users report fund loss to you.

Can a phishing domain impersonating my exchange be taken down?

Yes. Registrars, hosts, and ad networks will act on a properly evidenced takedown request, and a registered trademark strengthens that request considerably.

What can be done about fake support accounts on Telegram or Discord?

Platform impersonation reports plus evidenced takedown requests get fake accounts removed, typically within a day of confirmation once verified.

Does trademark registration help against on-chain or wallet-based impersonation?

Indirectly. It doesn't stop on-chain activity, but it strengthens takedown requests against the phishing domains, fake extensions, and impersonating accounts that drive users toward wallet-draining scams in the first place.

How fast can a fake exchange app or browser extension be removed?

Verified cases are typically actioned within 24 hours of confirmation, though app store and extension marketplace review times vary by platform.

How much does crypto brand protection cost?

Plans start at Monitor, pay-as-you-go, and scale up with your takedown volume across domains, extensions, and support channels. See Protection Plans for full pricing.

Do you monitor Telegram and Discord as well as the web?

Yes. Fake support accounts and impersonating admins on Telegram and Discord are covered by continuous monitoring, alongside domains, extensions, and app stores.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your brand name, primary domain, and any trademarks you already hold. We can start scanning from that alone.
